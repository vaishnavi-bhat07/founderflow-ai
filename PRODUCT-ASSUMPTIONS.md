FounderFlow AI is a web application designed to help startup founders manage their daily work in one place. It allows users to organise tasks, set goals, save ideas and track their progress. Our aim is to make startup management easier and help founders save time.
Starting a business involves many responsibilities, and founders often find it difficult to manage everything at once. They may struggle with planning tasks, meeting deadlines and deciding what to do first. FounderFlow AI brings these activities together in one simple platform.
Our application is mainly for startup founders, new entrepreneurs, small business owners and students with startup ideas. It helps them manage their daily activities, check pending tasks, plan their work and keep track of their goals.
The application will include a dashboard, task planner, goal tracker, idea vault and AI productivity assistant. The assistant will provide suggestions and help users divide bigger goals into smaller tasks.
We believe users prefer a simple platform where they can manage their work without switching between different applications. Features like task priorities and progress tracking can help them stay organised and save time.
Our MVP will include a landing page, dashboard, task management, goal tracking, idea management and a simulated AI assistant. Users can add tasks, mark them as completed, create goals and save ideas. We will use dummy data to make the application interactive.
